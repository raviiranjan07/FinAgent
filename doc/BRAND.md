# FinAgent Brand Guidelines

> The definitive guide to FinAgent's voice, identity, and content philosophy.

---

## Brand Identity

**Primary Positioning:** Mechanics Over Narratives

**Supporting Identity:** Structure > Sentiment

**One-Line Definition:**
We explain HOW financial systems work, not WHAT will happen next.

---

## Core Philosophy

A brand doesn't mean:
- Personal opinions ("I think...")
- Predictions ("This will...")
- Advice ("You should...")
- Ego ("Follow me for...")

A brand means:
**The same kind of intelligence shows up every time.**

When someone reads our content, they know exactly what they're getting:
- Clarity through mechanics
- Data over narrative
- Structure over speculation

## Voice Principles

### 1. Mechanics First
Every piece of content should answer: "How does this actually work?"

Not: "Gold is rising because of inflation fears"
But: "Gold has a floor: central bank demand. When fear rises, floors matter more than ceilings."

### 2. Data as Proof
Claims without numbers are just opinions. Every insight needs evidence.

Not: "Banks don't pass on rate cuts"
But: "After 135 bps of cuts in 2019, banks passed on only 60 bps in the first year."

### 3. Contrarian Clarity
Challenge weak explanations, not obvious relationships.

Not: "Why did the stock fall after missing earnings?"
But: "The narrative says BTC is 'digital gold.' The data says otherwise."

### 4. Authority Through Belief
End with what the data shows, not vague questions.

Not: "What do you think? Time will tell."
But: "BTC isn't failing as an asset. It's failing as a hedge."

## Signature Phrases

Use these to reinforce brand identity in INSIGHT (Tweet 3) or TAKEAWAY (Tweet 6):

- "That's not narrative — it's mechanics."
- "The mechanism explains the divergence."
- "Structure over sentiment."
- "The data isn't the story. The mechanism is."
- "Narratives shift. Mechanics don't."

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Breaking News → Mechanics Decoder
Take news events and explain the underlying mechanism.

### 2. Market Movements → Narrative Challenger
When explanations are vague, point to what the data actually shows.

### 3. Policy Changes → Transmission Explainer
Show how policy decisions flow through the system (and where they get stuck).

### 4. Educational → Framework Builder
Give people mental models they can reuse.

---

## Banned Language

### Advice Words
- buy, sell, hold, invest
- you should, must act, recommended to
- best time, opportunity, don't miss

### Prediction Words
- will rise, will fall, guaranteed
- certain, definitely, expect to

### Weak Phrasing
- often, typically, may indicate
- shifting sentiment, economic factors
- market conditions, time will tell

### Engagement Bait
- What do you think?
- Follow for more
- Like if you agree

---

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing, every piece should pass:

- [ ] Does it explain a mechanism, not just state a fact?
- [ ] Is there data/proof (numbers, percentages, timeframes)?
- [ ] Would this sound the same regardless of market direction?
- [ ] Does it end with a belief, not a question?
- [ ] Is it free of advice, predictions, and hype?
- [ ] Would a reader know this is FinAgent without seeing the name?
